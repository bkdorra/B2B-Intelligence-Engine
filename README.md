# B2B Intelligence Engine

### AI-Powered Company Research & Lead Generation Automation

An automation workflow designed to streamline B2B company research, extract relevant business information, and identify potential customers using AI.

Built with **Make.com, Google Gemini API, and Google Sheets**, the project combines workflow automation, AI-powered data analysis, and structured information extraction to turn company research into an organized and repeatable process.

## Key Features

- **Automated Research:** Collects company information and processes it through an automated workflow.
- **AI-Powered Analysis:** Uses Google Gemini to analyse company profiles and identify relevant business characteristics.
- **Lead Qualification:** Evaluates potential customers against predefined business criteria.
- **Structured Data Extraction:** Uses prompt engineering to generate consistent, machine-readable JSON outputs.
- **Automated Data Management:** Transfers processed company information into Google Sheets for further analysis and tracking.

## AI & Automation

A central component of the project is the integration of generative AI into an automated business workflow.

Custom prompts are designed to:

- Extract relevant information from company descriptions.
- Analyse business activities and potential customer needs.
- Assess alignment with predefined target customer criteria.
- Generate structured JSON responses for downstream processing.

The workflow connects AI-generated insights with automated data handling, reducing manual research and creating a repeatable lead qualification process.
